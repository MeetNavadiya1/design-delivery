## What is Design Delivery?
 
**Design Delivery** is a lightweight design review and approval platform built for creative agencies. Designers share work via a single link. Clients review, approve or reject — no login required. Every decision is captured, timestamped and stored. Nothing gets lost.
 
---
 
## The Problem
 
Creative agencies lose feedback every single day.
 
Work gets shared over WhatsApp. Approvals happen in email threads. Files live on Google Drive with no context. When a client says *"I never approved this"* — there's no proof. When a revision is requested — nobody remembers what changed or why.
 
The review cycle is broken. Feedback is scattered. Sign-offs are informal. And designers are left chasing clients across five different platforms just to get a yes or no.
 
---
 
## The Solution
 
Design Delivery brings the entire design review cycle into one place.
 
- **Agencies** create projects, add clients and assign tasks to designers
- **Designers** upload work and share a unique review link — via WhatsApp or email
- **Clients** click the link, view the design, and approve or reject with a comment — no account needed
- Every version, every feedback, every approval is **recorded and timestamped**
- If a design is rejected, the designer uploads a new version and the loop continues — cleanly, trackably, inside Design Delivery
No more lost threads. No more "I thought you approved this." No more chasing.
